# HR module — standard ERP HR (2026-10-09)

## Why
The HR module had payroll, leave, attendance and reimbursement pages, but the employee itself was only *(system user, employee id)*. There was no organisation structure, no leave policy (entitlements were hard-coded 14 / 7 / 7), a fixed 9–5 attendance rule, no holiday calendar and nothing about the person (contact, job, bank, statutory IDs, family, qualifications, documents, joining and leaving).

## Research
Compared the HR modules of ERPNext / Frappe HR, Odoo and SAP SuccessFactors:
- ERPNext: the Employee master holds personal, job, joining and leave details, with Employment Type, Department (tree, with leave approvers), Designation, Grade, Branch, Holiday List, Leave Policy, Shift Type as linked masters ([Employee](https://docs.frappe.io/hr/employee.md), [Department](https://docs.frappe.io/hr/department.md), [Designation](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/v12/user/manual/en/human-resources/designation), [overview](https://docs.erpnext.com/docs/v13/user/manual/en/human-resources)); onboarding and separation checklists, leave allocation and ledger, appraisals, recruitment.
- Odoo: Employees app (department hierarchy, contracts) with Time Off, Attendance, Recruitment and Appraisals apps ([overview](https://www.cybrosys.com/odoo/odoo-books/odoo-book-v13/human-resource/)).
- SAP SuccessFactors: Employee Central as the system of record (addresses, national IDs, salary), plus separate Onboarding / Offboarding, Recruiting and Performance modules ([overview](https://zalaris.com/resources/blog/sap-successfactors-modules-a-comprehensive-overview-for-hr-digitalisation)).

Common core in all three: employee master with sub-records, organisation masters, time and leave policy, onboarding / offboarding, HR reports. That core is what was built (scope chosen with you: **Core HR + Leave + Attendance**, Sri Lankan defaults). Recruitment, performance appraisals, training and grievances are the next layer and are **not** built yet.

## What was added
**Employee master** (`employees`, `GET/POST/PUT /employees`, `/employees/paged`)
- Personal: name with initials, NIC, passport, date of birth, gender, marital status, nationality, religion, blood group.
- Contact: personal email, mobile, home phone, address, permanent address.
- Job: department, designation, employment type, branch, reporting manager (loop-checked), grade, work location, shift, holiday calendar, joining / probation end / confirmation / contract end / retirement dates, notice period. Probation end and retirement (age 60) default automatically; contract types require an end date.
- Bank and statutory (EPF, ETF, TIN, NIC, passport) — behind the new `employee_sensitive` permission; hidden and untouchable without it.
- Lifecycle: status (active, probation, notice period, resigned, terminated, retired, inactive), **Confirm**, **Separate** (resign / terminate / retire / end of contract / death; optional login deactivation), **Reinstate**. No delete (history is kept).
- Sub-records: emergency contacts (one primary), dependents, education, work experience, documents (private storage, authenticated download, type and signature checks, expiry tracking), onboarding / offboarding checklists (standard templates, status per task).
- Created users automatically get an employee record with branch, default shift and default holiday calendar.

**Organisation and time masters** (`/hr/...`, page *HR Setup*): departments (tree, head), designations, employment types, shifts (hours, grace, working minutes, weekly off), holiday calendars with holidays (Sri Lanka fixed-date holidays seeded; poya days are added by HR each year).

**Leave**: leave types (code, days per year, accrual, carry-forward, paid / unpaid, half day, negative balance, max consecutive, minimum service, gender, medical certificate threshold) seeded with Sri Lankan statutory types; yearly **allocations** with generate-for-year (pro-rating for joiners, carry-forward caps, gender and service rules) and manual adjustment; balances per employee. Leave applications now enforce the policy: known type, working-day count (weekly offs and holidays excluded, server-computed via `/hr/leave-days`), half-day rules, overlap, balance, consecutive limit, no cross-year ranges, employee in service, and no self-approval (superusers exempt).

**Attendance**: late / early / overtime now follow the employee's shift (grace, working minutes) instead of a fixed 9–5.

**Reports and dashboard** (`/hr/reports/*`, page *HR Reports*, dashboard panel): headcount (department, designation, type, branch, gender, status), birthdays, anniversaries, expiring contracts / probation / retirements / documents, leave summary and who is on leave today, attrition (joiners, leavers, rate).

**Permissions** (new, migrated and granted to Admin plus the groups that already held the related permission): `hr_setup`, `hr_reports`, `leave_allocations`, `employee_sensitive`. Reference lists (departments, shifts, leave types ...) can be read by anyone who can view employees or leave.

## Technical notes
- Migration `s89_hr_core` (tables, employee columns, defaults, permissions, case-insensitive unique indexes); upgrade and downgrade tested.
- Code: `backend/app/modules/hr_core/` (models, schemas, service, reports, api); frontend `modules/hr/core-api.ts`, pages `HRSetupPage`, `EmployeesPage` (rewritten), `LeaveBalancesPage`, `HRReportsPage`, `components/MasterTab.tsx`, and updates to `LeavesPage` and `HRDashboard`.
- Tests: `backend/tests/test_hr_core.py` (72) plus adapted attendance tests; full suite 1103 passed, 2 failing (the known supplier-advance cashbook tests: `fn_insert_cashbook_entry` is missing from the dev database).
- Verified live in the browser: HR Setup (create), Employees (list, edit, family contact, checklist, leave tab, separation), Leaves (apply with server-computed days), Leave Balances, HR Reports, dashboard. Test data removed afterwards.

## Not built (next layer)
Recruitment (openings, applicants, interviews, offers), performance appraisals and goals, training, grievances, employee self-service portal, leave encashment, compensatory off, shift rosters / rotation, biometric device import, payroll reading the new bank / statutory fields (payroll still uses the salary profile).
