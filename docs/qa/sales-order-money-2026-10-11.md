# Sales Order money-integrity audit - 2026-10-11

Scope: API only (backend http://localhost:8020/api/v1, admin superuser). Every order, payment, settlement, cancellation and return was checked against exact Decimal arithmetic, the journal entries (JE) and general ledger (GL) rows in Postgres (read-only SQL), customer credit figures, reports, exports and the day-end reconciliation. Nothing was fixed or deleted.

Data: 93 QA orders in three QA branches (QA-BR-M 55, QA-BR-Q 37, QA-BR-N 1), 180 JEs, 11 sale returns, 14 credit settlements. Test ran 21:59-22:30 server time (credit sales are blocked after 18:00 for non-superusers, so credit orders were created by the superuser with `override_credit_validation=true`).

**Findings: 35 - Critical 7, High 11, Medium 12, Low 5.**

**Do the books balance overall? Mechanically yes, substantively no.** Every one of the 180 JEs balances (Dr = Cr), GL rows equal their JEs, no JE is duplicated, nothing posted for pending or cancelled orders, the trial balance balances (FY2026 Dr = Cr), and the day-end GL cash/bank movement equals the documents by payment method. But 9 completed orders have no revenue/cash posting at all (discount and coupon orders, see C1), gift-voucher sales are never posted (C7), credit notes can be over-redeemed or lost (C3, C4), partial returns over-refund (C5), and the Income Statement and Balance Sheet are wrong even when the ledger is right (C8, H1).

## Not tested (reason)

- Closed accounting period refusal: `accounting_periods` is empty and orders can only be dated "today" (no back-dating input); closing a global period would block the other tester's postings.
- Time near midnight: run finished about 22:30; created_date vs JE entry_date agreed for all orders.
- Credit-limit race (5 parallel orders at 60% of limit): non-superusers are blocked by the 09:00-18:00 rule and the superuser override skips both the limit check and the customer lock, so the locked path could not be driven. Only the validation endpoint (`credit-sale-validation?skip_time_check=true`) was exercised. Non-superuser use of `override_credit_validation` (expected 403) could not be re-tested: the Sales Manager test branch has no sellable stock.
- Cashbook vs GL: `cashbook_entries` is empty because the database has neither the `fn_insert_cashbook_entry` function nor the cashbook triggers (environment drift); day-end `is_balanced` is false for that reason only (cash difference = all GL cash/bank movement). Cashbook gaps are therefore not reported as findings.
- Debtors API (`/sales/debtors/*`): not routed (404), table `invoice_payments` does not exist; dead code.
- Percent-type or product-restricted coupons, gift voucher partial payment in split with credit note, agent commission amounts, multi-currency.

## Suggested fix order

1. Revenue JE: use `subtotal` as revenue (already net) and drop the second discount/coupon deduction; repost failures 590/591/598/599 and the discount JE rule (C1).
2. Returns: refund = line net (after line and header discount, tax-aware, inclusive prices) and stop writing refunds into `invoice.credit_note_amount` (C3, C4, H3, H9, H5); recompute customer outstanding after returns.
3. Credit notes and vouchers: lock the customer/credit note rows on redemption, ignore `gift_voucher_amount` without a voucher, commit after the voucher-sale GL hook (C2, C5, C6).
4. Reports: income statement sign of 4030, balance sheet asset mapping, status filters and returns in sales report, statistics and customer statement (C7, H1, H6, H7, M12).
5. Customer credit: exclude pending/rejected settlements from outstanding, int overflow on `left_credit_amount`, per-order total cap, override path lock (H4, M6, M7).
6. Advance application field in `InvoiceCreate` (H8), bank deposit voiding on reject (H10), settlement date validation (M5), `credit-settlements` 500 (M8), rounding policy (M1).
