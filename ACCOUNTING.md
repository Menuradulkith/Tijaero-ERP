### ðŸ”§ Post-Entry Adjustments (Corrections & Reversals)

After a journal entry is posted, the following corrective actions are available:

| Scenario | Action | How |
|----------|--------|-----|
| **Wrong amount or account** | **Correct** the JE | Finance > Journal Entries > select JE > **Correct**. System creates a reversal + new corrected entry. Requires `JOURNAL_ENTRY_UPDATE` permission. |
| **Full cancellation (void)** | **Reverse** the JE | Finance > Journal Entries > select JE > **Reverse**. Posts a mirror entry with opposite Dr/Cr, net effect = zero. |
| **Depreciation posted incorrectly** | Reverse depreciation JE, then repost correct amounts | Same reversal flow above, then create correct depreciation JE. |
| **Asset disposed** | Manual JE: clear asset + accumulated depreciation accounts; record gain/loss | `Dr Accumulated Depreciation`, `Cr Asset Account`. Then set asset status to `disposed` in asset register. |

> **Audit Trail:** All corrections, reversals, and depreciation entries store a `reference_id` pointing to the original JE. Descriptions contain the words **Correction**, **Reversal**, or **Depreciation** so they are easy to filter in reports.

## 1. Manual Journal Entries
These are created manually by the Admin/Accountant via `Finance > Accounting > Journal Entries`.

**Accountant Workflow:**
1. Review source documents (bank statements, cash receipts, opening balances).
2. Record a Manual Journal Entry with the correct debit and credit accounts.
3. Verify that debits equal credits and that the proper account codes are used.
4. Post the entry, which updates all related ledgers automatically.


### Initial Capital / Opening Balance
When the owner initially injects cash into the business or records starting balances.
- **Dr.** Cash in Hand / Bank Account (Asset)
- **Cr.** Opening Balance Equity / Owner's Equity (Equity)

### Owner Additional Investments
When the owner brings in more personal capital to fund operations.
- **Dr.** Cash in Hand / Bank Account (Asset)
- **Cr.** Owner's Equity (Equity)

### Year-End Closing (Manual or Assisted)
To close out net income into retained earnings.
- **Dr.** Net Income / Revenue Accounts
- **Cr.** Retained Earnings (Equity)

## 2. Automated System Journal Entries
These entries are automatically generated and posted to the General Ledger (GL) during operational workflows (Procurement, Sales, HR, etc.).

**Common Account Codes Reference:**
- `1010` - Cash in Hand / Petty Cash
- `1020` - Bank Account
- `1110` - Accounts Receivable (Trade Debtors)
- `1210` - Inventory Asset (Finished Goods)
- `2010` - Accounts Payable (Trade Creditors)
- `2020` - Supplier Advances
- `2520` - Customer Deposits (Advances)
- `4xxx` - Sales / Revenue Accounts
- `5xxx` - Expense Accounts (e.g., `5110` Salaries, `5150` Commission)

## 4. Foreign Exchange (FX) Revaluation / Currency Depreciation

Handling "depreciation on money" (i.e. losses or gains due to foreign currency exchange rate fluctuations) requires periodic revaluation of your foreign currency bank accounts, payables, and receivables. This ensures your financial statements reflect the current equivalent value in your base currency (LKR).

### When to Revalue
- **End of Month / Quarter / Year:** Revalue all foreign currency balances before closing the period.
- **Upon Settlement:** When a foreign invoice is paid and the realized exchange rate differs from the rate used when the invoice was booked.

### How to Record FX Adjustments (Manual Journal Entries)

If you hold a foreign currency asset (e.g. USD Bank Account) and the LKR depreciates (meaning the USD is now worth *more* LKR), you have a **Gain**. If the LKR appreciates (USD is worth *less* LKR), you have a **Loss**.

#### Scenario A: Foreign Currency Gain (LKR depreciates)
*Example: You hold ,000. It was booked at 300 LKR/USD (LKR 300,000). The month-end rate is 320 LKR/USD. The new value is LKR 320,000. You have an unrealized gain of LKR 20,000.*

1. **Go to:** Finance > Journal Entries > New Journal Entry
2. **Date:** Last day of the reporting period
3. **Entries:**
   - **Debit (Dr.):** 1020 Bank Account (or the specific foreign currency bank account) - LKR 20,000
   - **Credit (Cr.):** 8000 (or appropriate) FX Realized/Unrealized Gain Account - LKR 20,000
4. **Memo:** "Month-end FX revaluation for USD account @ 320 LKR/USD"

#### Scenario B: Foreign Currency Loss (LKR appreciates)
*Example: You owe a supplier ,000. Booked at 320 LKR/USD (LKR 320,000). At payment time, the rate is 330 LKR/USD. You have to pay LKR 330,000. You have a realized loss of LKR 10,000.*

1. **Go to:** Finance > Journal Entries > New Journal Entry
2. **Date:** Date of settlement
3. **Entries:**
   - **Debit (Dr.):** 8010 (or appropriate) FX Realized/Unrealized Loss Account - LKR 10,000
   - **Credit (Cr.):** 2000 Accounts Payable (or Bank if paying immediately) - LKR 10,000
4. **Memo:** "FX loss on settlement of invoice INV-123 @ 330 LKR/USD"
